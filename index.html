Quest Log

Chapter 38 - The Guild Of Our Lives

STORY SYNOPSIS

Vasate, a 24-year-old engineer trapped in childish urges of playing hollow blocks, accidentally causes a structural devastation to Boss Daemon's office building with a bulldozer. The company's economy deflates by $50,000. To redeem himself, Vasate must learn maturity, abandon childish pride, and embrace Good Manners And Right Conduct.

MORAL LESSON (GOOD MANNERS AND RIGHT CONDUCT)

"You need to move on and focus as time passes. If you want to care for your job, don't become childish. Become a wise adult in correct doings. Organize your life so no boss will judge you as immature. Be humble, friendly, sensitive, assertive, active, and pleasant. Use your coconut shell to practice Good Manners and Right Conduct!"
